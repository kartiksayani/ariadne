## Follow-ups

1. Core: queued never-prepared inputs stay bound to a retired binding after an
   explicit rebind. `delivery/claim.rs` filters by `binding_id`, so they are never
   claimable. Re-target them or let the owner move them (`bindings/mod.rs` ~547
   claims they stay queued in their original FIFO).
2. Recovery: an attempt with a committed result and unknown turn state can only be
   sealed by `skip` or `resend`. Add a "confirm completed" resolution.
3. Lifecycle: a session whose active binding is Disconnected cannot be closed
   (`history_actions/lifecycle.rs:127-132` only waives Paused).
4. Delivery format: `recent_context` grows to the 16 KiB cap per input. Cap it lower
   or dedupe to control host token cost.
5. Rules: `source.md` should say option `consequence` is required (3 rejected
   bootstrap applies).
6. Claude Mod: `$.plugin.root` is used but undocumented.
